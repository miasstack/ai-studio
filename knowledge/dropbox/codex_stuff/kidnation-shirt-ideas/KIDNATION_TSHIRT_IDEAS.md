# Kid Nation T-Shirt Ideas

These concepts use the existing Kid Nation kid art from `treasure-town-demo/assets/characters` and a temporary Kid Nation logo lockup. I did not find a standalone logo asset in the workspace, so the logo treatment in the mockups is a placeholder.

1. Crew Lineup
   All six kids shoulder-to-shoulder across the chest, big Kid Nation logo above, and the slogan "The Crew Runs the Block" underneath. Best shirt color: warm cream.

2. Pixel Power
   8-bit kid sprites on a dark grid with "Pixel Power" in arcade lettering. This is the most merch-friendly gamer version. Best shirt color: black or charcoal.

3. Block Party Tour
   The kids as a walking street crew under a "Block Party" sign, like a tour tee for a neighborhood adventure. Best shirt color: sky blue or washed white.

4. Future Builders Club
   Builder and captain art framed like club mascots with "Future Builders Club." Good for school, camp, STEM, and construction-play vibes. Best shirt color: oatmeal or heather gray.

5. Varsity Crew
   A collegiate Kid Nation lockup with three hero portraits in a badge. Feels like a premium hoodie or varsity tee. Best shirt color: navy.

6. Mystery Club
   Flashlight beam, noir circle badge, and a smaller group of kids as detectives. Good for story-driven drops. Best shirt color: black.

7. Brain Break Dance Crew
   Motion arcs, bright candy colors, and dancing kid sprites. This one fits music, videos, and movement-based Kid Nation content. Best shirt color: pale pink or white.

8. Neighborhood Heroes
   Shield crest packed with the kids and bold "Heroes" type. This is the strongest all-purpose brand shirt. Best shirt color: light blue or white.

9. Sticker Bomb
   Faces and character art scattered as die-cut stickers, with a simple black Kid Nation label. Great for kids because it feels collectible. Best shirt color: white.

10. World Builders
    Two kids over a globe/planet mark with "World Builders." Softer, aspirational, and parent-friendly. Best shirt color: mint or sage.

## Strongest Picks

Top 3 to develop first:

1. Neighborhood Heroes
2. Pixel Power
3. Sticker Bomb

Those three cover the main merch lanes: classic brand tee, gamer/cool kid tee, and playful collectible tee.

## Production Notes

- Convert final artwork to vector or high-resolution transparent PNGs before printing.
- Keep the logo as a separate layer so the real Kid Nation mark can replace the placeholder.
- For DTG printing, keep the full-color character art.
- For screen printing, simplify to 4-6 inks and use the 8-bit or badge concepts first.

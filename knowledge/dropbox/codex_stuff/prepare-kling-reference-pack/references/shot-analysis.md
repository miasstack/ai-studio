# Shot Analysis Checklist

Use this before prompting the image generator.
The goal is to preserve the source frame's shot design while swapping in Mia.

## Framing

- Aspect ratio
- Vertical or horizontal orientation
- Full body, three-quarter, waist-up, or close-up crop
- Amount of headroom
- How much space sits on each side of the subject

## Camera

- Camera height relative to the subject: low, eye level, high
- Camera tilt: neutral, up-tilted, down-tilted
- Lens feel: wide, normal, slightly telephoto

## Pose

- Torso angle
- Head angle
- Weight distribution
- Arm position
- Gaze direction
- Whether the subject faces camera, profile, or three-quarter

## Styling

- Outfit category: dress, blazer set, athleisure, swimwear, streetwear
- Silhouette: fitted, oversized, structured, flowing
- Fabrics and finish: satin, knit, denim, leather, cotton
- Comparable styling cues to preserve
- Elements that must be changed to avoid a literal copy

## Light and Environment

- Indoor or outdoor
- Key light direction
- Soft or hard light
- Background depth and blur
- Dominant palette
- Editorial mood: polished, candid, luxe, sporty, playful

## Mia Translation

Hold constant:

- Camera geometry
- Crop
- Pose direction
- Lighting logic
- Fashion category and energy

Adapt:

- Identity to Mia
- Outfit details
- Hair or makeup details when needed to fit Mia's look
- Logos, patterns, and copyrighted brand marks

# Seedance Prompt Templates

Use these as starting points, then customize for the user's subject, brand, song, or scene.

## Music video

`[artist/character] performs in [location], expressive body movement, [atmosphere], slow tracking shot, [lighting], cinematic music video aesthetic, emotional and polished, smooth motion, high detail, no text`

## Luxury product

`A [product] on [surface/background], subtle environmental movement, macro close-up with slow orbit camera, premium studio lighting, glossy textures, elegant and minimal, ad campaign look, ultra clean, no text or watermark`

## Fashion/editorial

`A model wearing [outfit] moves through [location], confident walk and turns, wind in fabric/hair, handheld mixed with slow dolly shots, editorial lighting, luxury fashion campaign feel, rich textures, high contrast, no text`

## Romantic cinematic

`Two lovers in [location] exchange a quiet look, gentle movement and natural gestures, soft push-in camera, warm practical lights, dreamy cinematic atmosphere, intimate and emotional, realistic detail, smooth motion`

## Street realism

`A young man in [wardrobe] walks through [street/location], passing traffic and city movement in the background, handheld camera with slight shake, available light, gritty realistic texture, urban and grounded, documentary-cinematic feel`

## Performance close-up

`Close-up of [subject] singing directly to camera, subtle head and hand movement, shallow depth of field, soft key light with rim light, emotionally charged performance, clean background, cinematic portrait quality`

## Dance / choreography

`A dancer performs [style] choreography in [location], full-body movement with flowing transitions, wide shot to medium tracking shot, dramatic directional lighting, rhythmic and energetic, crisp motion, high detail`

## Car / motion

`A [car] drives through [location] at [time of day], reflections moving across the body, low tracking camera and interior inserts, cinematic lighting, premium commercial look, speed and control, realistic detail`

## Food / drink

`A glass of [drink] is poured in close-up, condensation and splash detail, macro slow-motion feel, rich studio lighting, sensual textures, premium commercial aesthetic, clean background, no text`

## Prompt finishing tags

Append only what helps:

- `smooth motion`
- `cinematic lighting`
- `realistic textures`
- `shallow depth of field`
- `high detail`
- `no text, no watermark`

## Negative guidance

Use sparingly:

- `avoid extra fingers`
- `avoid warped facial features`
- `avoid floating objects`
- `avoid random text or logos`
- `avoid jittery motion`

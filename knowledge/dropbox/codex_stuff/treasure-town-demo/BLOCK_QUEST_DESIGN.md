# Block Quest

Kid-safe urban adventure for ages 6-9: bright open-block exploration, crew missions, scooter energy, hidden clues, upgrades, and light learning that feels like street-smart play instead of school.

## Core Fantasy

The player and the Kid Nation crew run the neighborhood by helping people, solving mysteries, racing routes, unlocking shortcuts, and upgrading safe hangout spots across the block.

## Kid Nation Crew

- Arjun: skatepark builder and scooter route planner.
- Melli: arcade clue finder and code breaker.
- Jordan: rooftop music and pattern specialist.
- Nari: subway shortcut scout and map decoder.
- Bjorn: food truck row organizer and snack economy helper.
- Salome: rec center leader and mission coordinator.

## First Playable

- One dense stylized city block.
- Six mission hotspots: Skatepark Sprint, Food Truck Row, Neon Arcade, Rooftop Remix, Subway Shortcut, Rec Center HQ.
- Tap-to-move exploration.
- Kid Nation character picker.
- Dialogue portraits from the existing Kid Nation assets.
- Three light-learning mission types: exact counts, clue words, beat/pattern reads.
- Rewards as Rep and Cash.
- District upgrades that make the block feel more alive.

## Educational Tone

Learning is embedded inside action:

- Counting means placing cones or snack boxes for a plan.
- Spelling means cracking a clue or cabinet code.
- Patterns mean keeping music, lights, routes, or crew moves in sync.

No quiz framing. Every challenge should feel like the crew is pulling off a clever neighborhood mission.

# KidNation First Video Brief

Use this as the first test render in Simplified.

## Goal

Create one realistic 20-second vertical UGC ad for KidNation using an adult parent spokesperson.

## Recommended settings

- Format: 9:16
- Length target: 18 to 22 seconds
- Persona: mom creator
- Tone: warm, practical, believable
- Background: bright kitchen or family room
- Gestures: subtle
- Delivery speed: medium
- Accent: neutral US English

## Script

Hook:
"I honestly didn't expect my kid to stick with this."

Full script:
"I honestly didn't expect my kid to stick with this, but KidNation has become part of our weekly routine. It keeps things fun, easy, and actually holds their attention. If you've been looking for something your kid will want to come back to, KidNation is worth checking out."

## On-screen text

- Line 1: "Didn’t expect this to stick"
- Line 2: "Now part of our weekly routine"
- End card: "KidNation"

## Avatar prompt

"Realistic adult mom creator, early 30s, casual but polished, natural makeup, soft expression, trustworthy and warm, filmed on smartphone, bright family kitchen, daylight, subtle hand gestures, direct eye contact, authentic UGC style, no exaggerated motion."

## Voice guidance

"Speak like a real parent making a recommendation to a friend. Keep it conversational, grounded, and slightly surprised in the first sentence."

## If the first render looks too AI

- Shorten the script by one sentence
- Reduce gesture intensity
- Lower enthusiasm by 15%
- Use a plainer background
- Slow speech slightly

## Shorter fallback script

"I honestly didn't expect my kid to stick with this, but KidNation surprised me. It fits into our week so easily, and they actually want to keep using it. If you're looking for something simple and fun, check out KidNation."

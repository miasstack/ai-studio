# Review Checklist

Use this checklist to approve or reject each generated video.

## Pass/Fail

Pass only if all of these are true:
- The avatar looks like a real adult, not uncanny
- Lip sync matches the voice closely
- The voice sounds natural and not over-robotic
- The script feels conversational, not like a brochure
- The first 2 seconds create curiosity
- The product or offer is understandable without extra context
- No claim sounds exaggerated or legally risky
- The CTA is clear
- The total runtime matches the intended placement
- No background elements feel fake or distracting

## Reject if you notice

- stiff head movement
- eyes drifting unnaturally
- too much smiling during serious lines
- overdone hand gestures
- rushed read speed
- obvious AI cadence
- kid-looking avatar used as the spokesperson
- lines that promise outcomes you cannot prove

## Simple scoring

Score each export from 1 to 5 on:
- realism
- hook strength
- clarity
- trust
- likelihood to stop scroll

Kill anything below:
- 4 on realism
- 4 on clarity
- 3 on hook strength

## Fast optimization loop

If realism is weak:
- shorten sentences
- remove slang
- reduce gesture intensity
- simplify the background

If the hook is weak:
- turn the first line into a direct opinion
- use a "didn't expect this" angle
- make the first sentence shorter

If the ad feels fake:
- replace brand language with personal language
- add a specific use case
- add a simple before/after contrast

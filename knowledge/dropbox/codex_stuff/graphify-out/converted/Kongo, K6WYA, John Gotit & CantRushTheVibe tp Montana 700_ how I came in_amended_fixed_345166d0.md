(2)	The Producer Royalty shall be calculated, determined, adjusted, reduced, paid and not paid pursuant to the terms set forth in the royalty provisions (“Royalty Provisions”) attached hereto as Exhibit “__”. The Producer Royalty for EPs, singles, foreign sales, Mid-price Records, Budget Records, non-USNRC Albums and other royalty-bearing sales and exploitations of records derived from, or uses of, the Master will be calculated, determined, adjusted, reduced, paid and not paid in the same proportion as Company’s royalties are calculated, determined, adjusted, reduced, paid and not paid pursuant to the Royalty Provisions.  Notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained herein, with respect to sales or other use of the Masters for which, pursuant to the Royalty Provisions, Company’s royalty is computed as a percentage of Distributor’s net receipts, net royalties or the like, Producer’s royalty hereunder in respect of such sale or use shall be equal to our royalty therefor (as calculated pursuant to the Royalty Provisions) multiplied by a fraction, the numerator of which is the Base Royalty and the denominator of which is Company’s basic rate in respect of full-priced, top-line Net Sales of USNRC Albums pursuant to the Royalty Provisions, provided that Producer’s share of net receipts, if any, derived from audio-visual works embodying a Master shall be fifty percent (50%) of the sum computed under the foregoing formula (and otherwise subject to the provisions of paragraph 4), and shall be payable on a prospective basis following recoupment of all production costs of such audio-visual works.

After recoupment of such Recording Costs as aforesaid, royalties shall be payable to Producer hereunder for all records sold embodying the Masters for which royalties are payable, retroactively from the first such record sold, subject to recoupment from such royalties of the Advance.  For the avoidance of doubt, Producer shall not earn any royalties in respect of any exploitation of any Master for which Company does not earn a royalty under the Royalty Provisions (e.g., without limitation, free goods) or otherwise.

(4)	As to records not consisting entirely of the Masters, the royalty rate otherwise payable to Producer hereunder with respect to sales of any such record shall be prorated by multiplying such royalty rate by a fraction, the numerator of which is equal to the number of Masters embodied on such record and the denominator of which is the total number of royalty bearing master recordings (including the Masters) embodied thereon.

(5)	In the event Company or Distributor engage the services of producers other than Producer in connection with any Master, then the royalty payable to Producer hereunder with respect to such Master shall be reduced by the royalty payable to such other producers.

(6)	Producer acknowledges and agrees that the advance and royalty participation set forth on Exhibit “A” will be the sole remuneration in respect of the services rendered by Producer in connection with the Master and the exploitation of the Master, and such constitutes fair and equitable remuneration for the services rendered (or to be rendered) by Producer and the rights granted herein.

F.	For a period of five (5) years following release of the Master, Producer agrees that Producer shall not remix, produce and/or coproduce, any recording for any person, firm or corporation other than Company which embodies, in whole or in part, any of the selections recorded in the Master

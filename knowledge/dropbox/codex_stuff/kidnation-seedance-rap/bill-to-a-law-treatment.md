# Bill to a Law — KidNation Music Video Treatment
## Full Seedance / Higgsfield Yaro Prompt Guide

---

## WORLD BIBLE

Style: 3D Pixar-style animation, vibrant primary colors, clean cinematic
lighting. Kid-safe civic-meets-hip-hop. The KidNation kids run the entire
government. Every location is slightly oversized and slightly ridiculous.
The kids are completely unbothered by any of it.

Characters:
- @mellysheet — Narrator. Sports-commentator host in a floating TV booth.
- @narisheet — Congresswoman (House). Genius, controlled chaos, idea person.
- @bjornsheet — House Rep. Passionate orator, commands any room he walks into.
- @salomesheet — Senator. Cool, measured, quietly the most powerful person there.
- @arjunsheet — Senate challenger. Skeptical then convinced then fully on board.
- @jordansheet — The President. Presidential swagger. Clearly a kid. Ready.

Audio setup:
- Upload each .mp3 clip from the /clips/ folder directly as @video_1 in Higgsfield.
- One clip per Seedance generation. Upload the matching clip before pasting the prompt.

Two-stage workflow:
- Stage 1 (Seedance): Generates character performance locked to the acapella vocal.
  Lip sync, expressions, and actions are all tied to specific words and lyric moments.
  Seedance has no instrumental — it cannot feel the beat drop. Do not write prompts
  that depend on drum hits or bass sync. Every visual cue must be tied to a lyric.
- Stage 2 (Post edit): All 20 Seedance clips get cut together against the full
  instrumental track. Beat sync happens here — the editor lands cuts on snare hits,
  transitions on the drop, scoreboards on the kick. That is not Seedance's job.

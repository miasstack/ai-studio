# Free Platform Recommendation

## Best fit right now

Use [Simplified](https://simplified.com/fr/ai-ugc-video-generator/free) as the first platform to test.

Why this is the best starting point:
- It is explicitly marketing a free-forever AI UGC video generator
- It is built around UGC-style video creation rather than generic text-to-video
- It includes AI actors, custom avatar uploads, and short-form social video workflows
- It is a better fit for high-volume ad-style iterations than cinematic text-to-video tools

The official page currently says:
- no credit card required
- free forever plan
- 90+ AI actors
- custom avatar uploads
- 4K exports

## Why not start with pure text-to-video tools

Tools that generate full scenes from prompts can look impressive, but they are usually slower and harder to control for:
- consistent spokesperson identity
- repeatable ad hooks
- direct-response pacing
- batch production

For "hyper realistic UGC," a talking-avatar or AI actor workflow is usually the fastest path.

## Suggested production workflow

1. Choose one creator persona per batch:
- mom creator
- dad creator
- teacher
- aunt
- after-school program lead

2. Keep each video between 15 and 30 seconds.

3. Use this structure:
- Hook: 2 to 5 seconds
- Problem or context: 4 to 8 seconds
- Demo or outcome: 6 to 10 seconds
- CTA: 2 to 5 seconds

4. Render 3 variants per script:
- calm and trustworthy
- excited and impressed
- practical and informative

5. Test square and vertical crops if the platform allows both.

## Quality note

For a kid/family brand, realism usually improves when you:
- use adult spokespersons
- keep background simple and believable
- avoid over-animated gestures
- use conversational lines instead of "ad copy" phrasing

## If Using Proof

Use a redacted rent ledger, bill, or notice only if you can remove:

- Name
- Address
- Landlord name
- Account number
- Phone numbers
- Email addresses
- Barcodes or QR codes

Leave visible if possible:

- Amount due
- Due date
- General category, such as rent or balance

## Image Generation Prompt For A Neutral Graphic

Create a clean, trustworthy fundraising cover image with no people and no personal information. Centered readable text says "Emergency Rent Bridge" and below it "$7,549 Past-Due Rent" and "Trying to Stay Housed." Use a calm off-white background, deep charcoal text, and one muted accent color. Minimal, modern, serious, high contrast, square format, no logos, no fake documents, no faces.

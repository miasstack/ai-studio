### Concept

Working title: Golden Retriever Leads Mia to Save a Puppy from a Crocodile

Mia is a Colombian river guide at a muddy jungle dock. The adult golden retriever runs to Mia, barking frantically. It leads her to a broken fishing skiff where a puppy is stranded while a crocodile circles nearby. Mia uses a pole and rope from her small rescue boat to pull the puppy to safety. The dogs remain the heroes because the adult golden discovers the danger and directs Mia every step of the way.

### Beat Sheet

1. Hook: puppy stranded on broken skiff, crocodile nearby, adult golden barking from shore
2. Alert: adult golden reaches Mia at the dock and urgently pulls her attention toward the river
3. Discovery: Mia sees the puppy and the crocodile and rushes into action
4. Rescue: Mia leans from a small boat with a rope pole system while the adult golden watches from the bow
5. Payoff: both dogs safe on shore with Mia hugging them
